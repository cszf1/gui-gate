## Claims

- **predicted_execution_failure**: measured - an executed step that errored or was immediately redone had a low predicted success beforehand (AUC, precision/recall at an intervention budget)
- **flagging_budget**: post_hoc_top_k_screen - the share b of test steps with the lowest predicted success, counted afterwards: a post-hoc top-k screen; it cannot be read as 'intervening on b % avoids x % of failures'
- **incremental_value**: needs_paired_branches - E[Y(o) - Y(direct) | h] is checked by incremental calibration on paired branches (bench/online_plan.py increment pilot); not from logs
- **avoided_failure_after_intervention**: not_estimable_from_logs - whether an intervention made a step succeed that would otherwise have failed; the refused step's outcome under direct is unobserved and the agent's next step is a different action. Needs paired counterfactual branches or a randomized online comparison (bench/online_plan.py)

"Avoided a failure because of the intervention" is not estimable from these logs and is not claimed.
